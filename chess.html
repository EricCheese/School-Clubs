Chess Club

Instructed By Mr. Fonseca & Mr. Pech

Description: The Chess Club is a community where people of all skill levels can meet in person to play, learn and enjoy a game of chess.

Goal: The Chess Club aims to provide its members with problem-solving, critical thinking, and strategic planning.

Here are some of the activities the Chess Club hosts.

Weekly Tournaments

This weekly event is to have members prove their skill-level.

Game Analysis

Every game, every move is analyzed to better one's abilities.

Presenting New Moves

Chess isn't limited to a single opening, or move. Share your plans to inspire others.
