Environmental Club

Instructed By Ms. Paredez & Ms. Gomez

Description: The Environmental Club is a community where people can learn more about the environment

Goal: The Chess Club aims to educate its members in climate issues worldwide.

Here are some of the activities the Environmental Club hosts.

Monthly Field Trips

This monthly event can provide members with insight to issues that other places may experience.

Presenting Possible Solutions

Members are encouraged to think and find solutions that may help with other environments.

Requirements:

Must have a GPA of 3.5 and above.

Must have no disciplinary actions prior.
