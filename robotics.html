Robotics Club

Instructed By Mr. Silva & Mr. Sosa

Description: The Robotics Club is a community where people can see how real-world robots are programmed.

Goal: The Robotics Club aims to provide its members with insight on how they can make their own solutions using robotics.

Here are some of the activities the Robotics Club hosts.

Weekly Competitions

This weekly event is to have members prove their skill-level in terms of programming

Design Analysis

Every robot, every code is analyzed to better one's abilities in the field.

Requirements

Must have a GPA of 3.5 and above.

Must have no disciplinary actions on record prior.
